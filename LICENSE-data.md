### Not medical advice

This is a signposting directory. It does not diagnose, triage, assess risk, or recommend
treatment, and it must not be presented as doing any of those. Do not wrap it in an interface
that implies clinical judgement it does not have.

### Listing is not endorsement

Inclusion means a service was found and recorded. It does not mean it was vetted, accredited,
licensed, quality-assured or recommended, and the ordering carries no ranking. Do not present
entries as approved, verified, certified or rated by us, and do not present the compiler's

than one person's opinion.

### Do not republish stale crisis numbers

Every export carries `export.source_last_updated`. Phone numbers change, helplines close, and
hours move. If you republish a number, publish the date alongside it, and re-pull rather than
freezing a copy. A wrong number given to someone in crisis is a concrete harm, and it is the
one failure mode of this dataset that actually hurts people.

If you cannot commit to refreshing, link to the entry rather than copying the number.

### Do not imply completeness

The directory is not exhaustive and does not claim to be. Some exports deliberately withhold a
small, disclosed set of non-crisis entries as an experimental control — see
`export.withheld_note`. Do not present a copy as "all mental-health services in Israel".
